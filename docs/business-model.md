# Business Model: Messenger, Package Delivery and Luggage Porterage Route Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9621`
- ISCO-08: `9621`
- Occupation: Messengers, Package Deliverers and Luggage Porters
- Social impact: worker-safety, delivery-reliability, logistics-continuity

## Customer

- courier and last-mile delivery operators
- hotel/transport-terminal luggage-portering services
- independent messenger and package-delivery crews / delivery cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/route task scheduling coordination
- delivery-log/progress-record logging
- delivery-equipment/consumables procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to route safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a delivery-execution decision (e.g.
  authorizing a specific delivery route/luggage-handling operation),
  ever
- no direct finalization of a route-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a route cleared for safety), ever
- no override of a route safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (delivery-traffic hazard, manual-lifting
  hazard, equipment-condition hazard) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
